# Reference photography (William's own work)

The one class of reference image the forge may use without a second thought: William's photographs. They
stay where they are — **nothing from these folders is copied into this public repo**; jobs refer to them by
path, and only to frames William has picked for a given purpose. Not training data for a general style:
*lighting and place references* for specific scenes, fed to reference-capable models (FLUX.2 klein
multi-reference, Qwen-Image-Edit, SDXL + IP-Adapter) and, if they prove out, a small lighting LoRA scoped to
one character.

## Shot list William could shoot for the forge (Nokesville and the road)
Lighting plates and places, not finished art — each becomes a concept→kit source or a lighting reference:
1. A rural gas station canopy at dusk and at full dark (fluorescent canopy, sodium lot light) — Dry Fork, S-A04.
2. A farmhouse kitchen at breakfast, window light from the left — card 01 of the Gate Law and every "home" interior.
3. A two-lane Virginia road at golden hour and at night from the driver's seat — the road-trip overworld look.
4. A small-town main street, church, diner, hardware store — Linden Mills' kit.
5. A high-school crosswalk and front steps — Slice A's school street.
6. Single practical sources as plates: candle, oil lamp, phone screen in a dark room, a TV's glow, a bare bulb — the night-lighting law's vocabulary.
7. Textures at one square metre, straight down, flat light: asphalt, gravel, siding, brick, linoleum, church-basement tile — `tile_texture` references.
